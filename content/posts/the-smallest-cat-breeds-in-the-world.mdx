Most domestic cats weigh between 8 and 12 pounds, but some breeds remain surprisingly small even as adults. These tiny felines may be small in size, but they make up for it with huge personalities.

Whether you're looking for a compact companion for apartment living or simply love adorable miniature cats, these breeds are among the smallest in the world.

Let's meet the tiniest cats on the planet.

## 1. Singapura

![Singapura cat](/uploads/smallestcatbreeds2.webp)

The Singapura is widely recognized as the smallest domestic cat breed in the world. Despite its tiny frame, it is energetic, curious, and incredibly affectionate.

### Size

- Weight: 4–8 pounds
- Height: 6–8 inches
- Length: 12–15 inches

### Personality

- Curious
- Playful
- Affectionate
- Social

### Fun Fact

The Singapura is often called the "little lion of love" because of its affectionate nature.

---

## 2. Munchkin

![Munchkin cat with short legs](/uploads/smallestcatbreeds3.webp)

The Munchkin is famous for its short legs, which result from a natural genetic mutation. Its unique appearance has made it one of the most recognizable cat breeds.

### Size

- Weight: 5–9 pounds
- Height: 5–7 inches
- Length: 12–16 inches

### Personality

- Friendly
- Outgoing
- Playful
- Intelligent

### Fun Fact

Munchkins often sit upright on their hind legs like meerkats to observe their surroundings.

---

## 3. Devon Rex

![Devon Rex cat with curly coat](/uploads/smallestcatbreeds4.webp)

With its large ears, curly coat, and mischievous personality, the Devon Rex looks unlike almost any other cat breed.

### Size

- Weight: 5–10 pounds
- Height: 8–10 inches
- Length: 15–18 inches

### Personality

- Energetic
- Curious
- Affectionate
- Entertaining

### Fun Fact

The Devon Rex is often nicknamed the "pixie cat" because of its elf-like appearance.

---

## 4. Cornish Rex

![Cornish Rex cat with soft wavy coat](/uploads/smallestcatbreeds5.webp)

The Cornish Rex is slender, lightweight, and covered with soft, wavy fur. Its elegant body structure makes it appear even smaller than it actually is.

### Size

- Weight: 5–10 pounds
- Height: 8–12 inches
- Length: 15–18 inches

### Personality

- Active
- Athletic
- Friendly
- Intelligent

### Fun Fact

Unlike most cats, the Cornish Rex only has a soft undercoat, giving its fur a velvet-like texture.

---

## 5. American Curl

![American Curl cat with curled ears](/uploads/smallestcatbreeds6.webp)

The American Curl is famous for its distinctive ears that curl backward. It remains relatively small throughout its life.

### Size

- Weight: 5–10 pounds
- Height: 8–10 inches
- Length: 12–18 inches

### Personality

- Gentle
- Curious
- Playful
- Adaptable

### Fun Fact

Kittens are born with straight ears, which gradually curl backward as they grow.

---

## 6. Siamese

![Siamese cat with slender body](/uploads/smallestcatbreeds7.webp)

Although not the absolute smallest breed, the Siamese has a naturally slim body and lightweight frame.

### Size

- Weight: 6–10 pounds
- Height: 8–10 inches
- Length: 15–20 inches

### Personality

- Vocal
- Intelligent
- Social
- Loyal

### Fun Fact

Siamese cats are known for carrying on long "conversations" with their owners.

---

## 7. Balinese

![Balinese cat with long silky coat](/uploads/smallestcatbreeds8.webp)

The Balinese resembles a long-haired Siamese and is admired for its graceful appearance.

### Size

- Weight: 5–10 pounds
- Height: 8–10 inches
- Length: 15–20 inches

### Personality

- Friendly
- Intelligent
- Active
- Affectionate

### Fun Fact

Despite its elegant coat, the Balinese requires surprisingly little grooming.

---

## 8. Japanese Bobtail

![Japanese Bobtail cat](/uploads/smallestcatbreeds9.webp)

The Japanese Bobtail is a lightweight breed known for its rabbit-like tail.

### Size

- Weight: 5–10 pounds
- Height: 8–9 inches
- Length: 12–15 inches

### Personality

- Energetic
- Smart
- Social
- Curious

### Fun Fact

No two Japanese Bobtail tails are exactly alike.

---

## 9. Burmese

![Burmese cat with compact muscular body](/uploads/smallestcatbreeds10.webp)

Burmese cats are compact and muscular, often feeling heavier than they appear.

### Size

- Weight: 6–10 pounds
- Height: 8–10 inches
- Length: 14–18 inches

### Personality

- Loving
- Playful
- People-oriented
- Confident

### Fun Fact

Burmese cats are sometimes called "velcro cats" because they love staying close to their owners.

---

## 10. Oriental Shorthair

![Oriental Shorthair cat](/uploads/smallestcatbreeds11.webp)

The Oriental Shorthair has a sleek body, large ears, and a lightweight frame that places it among the smaller cat breeds.

### Size

- Weight: 5–10 pounds
- Height: 8–10 inches
- Length: 15–20 inches

### Personality

- Active
- Talkative
- Intelligent
- Curious

### Fun Fact

Oriental Shorthairs come in more than 300 color and pattern combinations.

## Why Are Some Cat Breeds So Small?

![Why some cat breeds remain small infographic](/uploads/smallestcatbreeds14.webp)

Several factors influence a cat's size:

### Genetics

Certain breeds naturally inherit smaller body structures from their ancestors.

### Selective Breeding

Breeders often preserve specific traits, including compact size.

### Bone Structure

Small breeds usually have lighter skeletons and slimmer builds compared to larger cats.

## Are Small Cat Breeds Good Pets?

![Small cat breeds for apartment living](/uploads/smallestcatbreeds15.webp)

Small cat breeds are excellent companions because they are:

- Easier to carry and handle
- Well-suited for apartments
- Generally consume less food
- Great for first-time cat owners

However, they still need the same level of care, attention, and veterinary support as larger cats.

## Final Thoughts

Tiny cat breeds prove that great things come in small packages. Whether it's the miniature Singapura, the short-legged Munchkin, or the playful Devon Rex, these cats offer plenty of personality despite their compact size.

If you're looking for a cat that's small enough to fit comfortably into apartment life while still providing endless companionship, one of these miniature breeds may be the perfect choice.
