# Cat Breeds That Shed the Least Hair

One of the biggest concerns for potential cat owners is shedding. While no cat is completely shed-free, some breeds lose significantly less hair than others. These low-shedding cats can make cleaning easier and may be a better choice for people who dislike finding fur on furniture, clothes, and carpets.

If you're searching for a feline companion with minimal shedding, these breeds deserve a closer look.

## Do Any Cats Not Shed?

The short answer is no.

All cats shed to some degree. However, certain breeds shed much less because they have little fur, a unique coat structure, or slower hair growth cycles.

Let's explore the cat breeds that shed the least hair.

---

## 1. Sphynx

![Sphynx Cat](/images/sphynx-low-shedding.jpg)

The Sphynx is often considered the ultimate low-shedding cat because it has almost no fur.

### Why It Sheds So Little

- Nearly hairless body
- No thick undercoat
- Minimal loose fur

### Key Characteristics

- Weight: 6–12 pounds
- Lifespan: 8–14 years
- Energy Level: High
- Personality: Social and affectionate

### Grooming Needs

Although they don't shed much, Sphynx cats require regular baths because natural skin oils aren't absorbed by fur.

### Fun Fact

A Sphynx feels warm and soft, similar to suede or peach fuzz.

---

## 2. Devon Rex

![Devon Rex Cat](/images/devon-rex-low-shedding.jpg)

The Devon Rex is famous for its large ears, playful personality, and soft curly coat.

### Why It Sheds So Little

- Very short coat
- Minimal guard hairs
- Lightweight fur structure

### Key Characteristics

- Weight: 5–10 pounds
- Lifespan: 10–15 years
- Energy Level: High
- Personality: Playful and intelligent

### Grooming Needs

Occasional brushing is usually enough to keep the coat healthy.

### Fun Fact

The Devon Rex is often called the "pixie cat" because of its unique appearance.

---

## 3. Cornish Rex

![Cornish Rex Cat](/images/cornish-rex-low-shedding.jpg)

The Cornish Rex has one of the most unusual coats in the cat world.

### Why It Sheds So Little

- Lacks outer guard hairs
- Soft curly undercoat
- Lightweight fur

### Key Characteristics

- Weight: 5–10 pounds
- Lifespan: 11–15 years
- Energy Level: High
- Personality: Active and affectionate

### Grooming Needs

Minimal grooming is needed because the coat rarely mats.

### Fun Fact

The Cornish Rex coat feels similar to velvet.

---

## 4. Russian Blue

![Russian Blue Cat](/images/russian-blue.jpg)

The Russian Blue has a dense coat but surprisingly sheds less than many other double-coated breeds.

### Why It Sheds So Little

- Stable coat growth cycle
- Less seasonal shedding
- Dense but manageable fur

### Key Characteristics

- Weight: 7–15 pounds
- Lifespan: 15–20 years
- Energy Level: Moderate
- Personality: Gentle and loyal

### Grooming Needs

Weekly brushing is usually sufficient.

### Fun Fact

The Russian Blue's coat often appears silver-blue under sunlight.

---

## 5. Siamese

![Siamese Cat](/images/siamese-low-shedding.jpg)

The Siamese has a short, sleek coat that produces relatively little shedding.

### Why It Sheds So Little

- Fine, short hair
- No thick undercoat
- Easy-to-maintain coat

### Key Characteristics

- Weight: 6–14 pounds
- Lifespan: 12–20 years
- Energy Level: High
- Personality: Vocal and affectionate

### Grooming Needs

Occasional brushing helps remove loose hairs.

### Fun Fact

Siamese cats are known for their striking blue eyes.

---

## 6. Oriental Shorthair

![Oriental Shorthair Cat](/images/oriental-shorthair-low-shedding.jpg)

The Oriental Shorthair is closely related to the Siamese and shares its low-shedding coat.

### Why It Sheds So Little

- Short, fine fur
- Minimal undercoat
- Easy maintenance

### Key Characteristics

- Weight: 5–10 pounds
- Lifespan: 12–15 years
- Energy Level: High
- Personality: Curious and social

### Grooming Needs

A quick weekly brushing is usually enough.

### Fun Fact

Oriental Shorthairs come in hundreds of color combinations.

---

## 7. Burmese

![Burmese Cat](/images/burmese-low-shedding.jpg)

The Burmese has a smooth coat that requires very little maintenance.

### Why It Sheds So Little

- Fine coat texture
- Short fur length
- Minimal loose hair

### Key Characteristics

- Weight: 6–12 pounds
- Lifespan: 14–18 years
- Energy Level: Moderate
- Personality: Loving and playful

### Grooming Needs

Little more than occasional brushing.

### Fun Fact

Burmese cats often behave more like dogs than cats.

---

## 8. Bombay

![Bombay Cat](/images/bombay-cat.jpg)

The Bombay is known for its glossy black coat and panther-like appearance.

### Why It Sheds So Little

- Short coat
- Minimal undercoat
- Low maintenance fur

### Key Characteristics

- Weight: 6–11 pounds
- Lifespan: 12–16 years
- Energy Level: Moderate
- Personality: Friendly and outgoing

### Grooming Needs

Weekly brushing is typically enough.

### Fun Fact

Bombays are sometimes called "mini panthers."

---

## 9. Peterbald

![Peterbald Cat](/images/peterbald-low-shedding.jpg)

The Peterbald may be hairless or possess an extremely short coat.

### Why It Sheds So Little

- Little to no fur
- Minimal hair growth
- Almost no loose hair

### Key Characteristics

- Weight: 6–12 pounds
- Lifespan: 12–15 years
- Energy Level: High
- Personality: Intelligent and affectionate

### Grooming Needs

Skin care is more important than coat care.

### Fun Fact

Some Peterbald kittens lose their coats as they mature.

---

## 10. Bengal

![Bengal Cat](/images/bengal-low-shedding.jpg)

The Bengal's short, fine coat sheds far less than many long-haired breeds.

### Why It Sheds So Little

- Tight coat structure
- Short hair length
- Less frequent shedding

### Key Characteristics

- Weight: 8–15 pounds
- Lifespan: 12–16 years
- Energy Level: Very High
- Personality: Active and intelligent

### Grooming Needs

Minimal brushing is required.

### Fun Fact

Bengal coats often have a glitter-like shine.

## Are Low-Shedding Cats Hypoallergenic?

Not exactly.

Many people believe low-shedding cats are hypoallergenic, but cat allergies are usually triggered by a protein called **Fel d 1**, which is found in saliva, skin, and dander—not just fur.

Low-shedding cats may spread fewer allergens around the home, but they are not guaranteed to be allergy-free.

## Tips for Reducing Cat Hair in Your Home

Even with a low-shedding breed, you can further reduce loose fur by:

- Brushing regularly
- Using air purifiers
- Vacuuming frequently
- Providing a healthy diet
- Keeping your cat hydrated
- Scheduling regular veterinary checkups

## Which Low-Shedding Cat Is Right for You?

### Choose a Sphynx if:
You want the least shedding possible and don't mind regular skin care.

### Choose a Devon Rex or Cornish Rex if:
You want a playful cat with a unique curly coat.

### Choose a Russian Blue if:
You prefer a calm and elegant companion.

### Choose a Siamese if:
You enjoy an interactive and vocal pet.

### Choose a Bengal if:
You want a low-shedding cat with an exotic appearance.

---

## Final Thoughts

If shedding is a major concern, several cat breeds can help keep fur around the house to a minimum. From the nearly hairless Sphynx to the sleek Siamese and curly-coated Devon Rex, these cats combine beauty, personality, and low-maintenance coats.

While no cat is completely shed-free, choosing one of these breeds can significantly reduce the amount of fur you'll find on your furniture, clothing, and floors.
